# MessMate project brief

## Team
Team Tiffin: Kabir Singh and Neha Joshi.

## Problem
Hostel residents have no quick way to rate the day's meals, so the mess committee hears complaints
only at the monthly meeting.

## Plan
A mobile-friendly page where residents rate each meal in two taps and leave an optional comment; the
committee sees a daily summary.
