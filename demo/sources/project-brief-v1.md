# FestPass project brief

## Programme
Team Lantern is building FestPass for Campus Build Sprint 2026, the two-week programme run by the
college Innovation Cell.

Faculty coordinator: Prof. Meera Kulkarni, Innovation Cell.

## Problem
Registrations for Autumn Fest still run on paper forms and group-chat polls. Students register twice
for the same event, forms go missing, and organisers can't tell how many seats are left until the
doors open.

## Goals
- A student can find an event and register in under a minute.
- Every registration produces a QR pass that volunteers scan at the door.
- Organisers see a live seat count for each event.

## Team
Ananya Iyer, Rohan Mehta, Ishita Rao and Dev Malhotra, all second-year students.

## Timeline
Final submissions close on 20 September 2026.

## Budget
Budget cap: ₹50,000 (Innovation Cell grant).

## Out of scope
Payments for paid workshops, and events outside Autumn Fest.
