## Update 4 · Judging schedule
Campus Build Sprint, organiser update 4, from Sana Sheikh, 17 September 2026. Judging takes place on
23 September from 10:00 to 16:00 in the Innovation Cell. Each team gets an 8-minute slot: five minutes
to demo and three for the judges' questions. Slots are emailed the evening before.

## Update 4 · Submission checklist
A complete submission has four parts: the repository link, a demo video of at most three minutes, the
deployed URL, and a README that explains how to run the project. A submission missing any part is
marked incomplete and isn't judged.

## Update 4 · Deadline reminder
A reminder that submissions close on 22 September at 23:59 IST. The form locks automatically at that
time and late submissions are not accepted, whatever the reason, so please don't wait for the last
hour.

## Update 4 · Robotics Expo clash
Teams also entering the Inter-College Robotics Expo should note that expo entries close on 21
September. The expo is a separate event with its own form, and a Campus Build Sprint submission does
not count as an expo entry.

## Update 4 · Prizes
The first prize is ₹30,000 and the runner-up gets ₹15,000. The top three teams are also invited to
interviews for the Innovation Cell's incubation programme in October.
