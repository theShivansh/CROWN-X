## Volunteers · Recruitment
Autumn Fest needs 40 volunteers across the three days. The volunteer sign-up form is shared in every
hostel group and closes on 19 September at 20:00. Sana Sheikh confirms the final list and sends the
shift allocation two days later.

## Volunteers · Shifts
Each fest day has three shifts: 08:00 to 12:00, 12:00 to 16:00 and 16:00 to 20:00. A volunteer takes
at most two shifts a day, and nobody works the first and the last shift on the same day.

## Volunteers · Door duty
Two volunteers stand at each open gate with a scanner phone. If a phone's scanner stops working, the
volunteer checks the student's roll number against the printed list for that gate and writes the time
next to it, and the entry is added to FestPass later.

## Volunteers · Briefing
All volunteers attend a briefing on 24 September at 17:00 in Seminar Hall 2. It covers the scanner
app, the gates, what to do when the queue backs up, and who to call when something breaks.

## Volunteers · Perks
Every volunteer gets a fest T-shirt, a meal coupon for each shift and a certificate signed by Prof.
Meera Kulkarni. Volunteers who complete four or more shifts also get priority seats at the closing
concert.
