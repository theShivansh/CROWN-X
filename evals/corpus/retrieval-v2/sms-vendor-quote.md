## SMS quote · Vendor and pricing
Quote from Kavach SMS, received 8 September 2026, for one-time password messages at registration.
Each OTP message costs ₹0.18, so a pack of 50,000 message credits comes to ₹9,000. Credits are valid
for twelve months and unused credits are not refunded.

## SMS quote · Throughput limits
Kavach SMS accepts up to 10 messages per second per sender ID. Anything above that is queued for up to
two minutes and then rejected. A second sender ID can be added for ₹1,500 a year if the fest needs more
throughput on the first morning.

## SMS quote · Delivery and retries
An OTP stays valid for five minutes. The student can ask for it to be sent again after 30 seconds, and
at most three times per registration. Delivery reports come back through a webhook within ten seconds
for most Indian networks.

## SMS quote · DLT registration
Under TRAI rules every message template and the sender ID must be registered on the DLT portal before
the first message is sent. Kavach SMS says approval usually takes three working days, and messages
using an unregistered template are silently dropped by the operators.

## SMS quote · Invoice terms
Kavach SMS invoices the credit pack in advance. Payment is due within 15 days of the invoice date and
GST at 18% is charged on top of the quoted price. The invoice goes to the Innovation Cell's accounts
office, not to the team.
