## Venue · Main stage
The main stage is the open-air amphitheatre behind the library, with a seating capacity of 1,200.
Cultural events and the closing concert are held there. Seats are not numbered, so FestPass counts
entries rather than assigning seats.

## Venue · Workshop rooms
Workshops run in Labs 3 and 4 of the Computer Science block, with 60 seats each. Paid workshops use
Lab 4 only, because it has the projector that the workshop speakers asked for.

## Venue · Gates
Fest entry is through Gate 1, the main gate, and Gate 3 on the hostel side. Gate 2 stays closed for
construction until November, so no scanner is assigned to it.

## Venue · Power and network
Campus Wi-Fi does not reach the amphitheatre reliably, and mobile data slows down when the crowd is
large. Each gate gets a 4G hotspot and a power bank for the scanner phones, and the organisers'
control desk has a wired connection in the library.

## Venue · Fest dates
Autumn Fest runs from 25 to 27 September 2026. Gates open at 09:00 each day and the last event ends at
21:00, except on the closing night, when the concert runs until 22:30.
