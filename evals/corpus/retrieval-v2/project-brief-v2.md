## Brief v2 · Programme and sponsor
FestPass project brief, version 2, dated 15 September 2026. Team Lantern is building FestPass for
Campus Build Sprint 2026, the two-week programme run by the college Innovation Cell. The faculty
coordinator is Prof. Meera Kulkarni and the student coordinator is Sana Sheikh. Following organiser
update 3, final submissions now close on 22 September 2026 instead of 20 September.

## Brief v2 · Problem
Registrations for Autumn Fest still run on paper forms and group-chat polls. Students register twice
for the same event, forms go missing between the stalls and the office, and organisers can't tell how
many seats are left until the doors open. Last year two workshops were overbooked by more than thirty
people each and had to turn students away at the door.

## Brief v2 · Goals and success measures
A student can find an event and register in under a minute from a phone. Every registration produces
a QR pass that volunteers scan at the gate. Organisers see a live seat count for each event. Success
for the fest week means 2,000 registrations handled without a paper form and no event overbooked.

## Brief v2 · Scope changes since v1
Version 1 left payments out of scope. Version 2 brings paid workshops into scope: FestPass will collect
workshop fees through a payment gateway, starting with its sandbox during the sprint. Events outside
Autumn Fest stay out of scope, and so do refunds, which organisers will keep handling by hand.

## Brief v2 · Budget and grant
The Innovation Cell cut its grant by 10%, so the budget cap falls from ₹50,000 in version 1 to
₹45,000. The line items live in the budget sheet, which is the source of truth for amounts. Any spend
above ₹5,000 on a single item needs Ananya Iyer's approval before the order is placed.
